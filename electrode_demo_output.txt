Electrode State Demonstration
============================================================
Initial state: All electrodes normal

Simulating electrode interactions:
-----------------------------------
1. Click electrode R1 (independent)
   → R1 becomes active (red)

2. Click electrode P1_1 (parallel group)
   → P1_1 and P1_2 both become active (synchronized)

3. Click electrode C10 (channel)
   → C10 becomes active (individual)

Parallel electrode behavior:
- Clicking any electrode in a parallel group toggles the entire group
- This ensures synchronized control of related electrodes

Color coding:
  🔵 Blue - Parallel electrodes (normal state)
  🟢 Teal - Independent electrodes (normal state)
  ⚪ Gray - Channel electrodes (normal state)
  🔴 Red  - Any electrode in active state
